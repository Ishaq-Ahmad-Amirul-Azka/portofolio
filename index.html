Strategy first. Craft that carries it.

I'm Wren — a senior creative and marketing specialist based in Portland, currently leading brand and campaign work at Field & Co. Studio. Before that, I cut my teeth at Northlight Agency and Coil Creative, working across retail, food & beverage, fintech, and a long list of startups that needed a point of view fast.

My approach is simple: get the strategy right before anyone touches a layout tool, then hold the craft to a standard that makes the strategy obvious. I've run workshops with founders, art-directed shoots, written the headline, and presented the performance report — often in the same week.

  • Strategy before style

    Every campaign starts with a clear position and a reason to believe. Design and copy exist to carry that argument, not decorate it.

  • Craft over noise

    I'd rather ship one sharp idea than five safe ones. Consistency and restraint build recognition faster than volume does.

  • Numbers that matter

    Every brief ends with a metric that mattered before it starts. Vanity numbers don't make the final report.
